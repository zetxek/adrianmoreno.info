# Processor record — Resend

**Assessed:** 2026-07-25
**Purpose:** Newsletter subscriber storage and email delivery
**Role:** Processor (site owner is controller)
**Status:** Assessed and approved for use. DPA accepted via Terms of Service.

## What data it processes

| Data | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Subscriber submits via `/newsletter/` or footer form | Delivering the newsletter |
| `confirmed_at` timestamp | Written by `/api/confirm` on double opt-in | Proof of consent (GDPR Art. 7(1)) |
| Open / click events | Generated by Resend | Delivery diagnostics |
| Bounce / complaint events | Generated by Resend | List hygiene, deliverability |

No name, no IP address collected by us at signup, no other identifiers. Resend acts
as an independent controller for its own account and usage data — see DPA §9.

## GDPR position

**DPA:** <https://resend.com/legal/dpa> — last updated 31 December 2025.

Structure: 12 sections plus three exhibits (A: processing details and retention,
B: party identification and importer/exporter roles, C: technical and organisational
security measures). Notable clauses: sub-processors permitted with 14 days' notice
(§4), audit rights and breach notification (§8), Resend as independent controller for
account data (§9).

**Execution:** click-accepted. The DPA becomes binding on acceptance of the Terms of
Service; there is no separate signature step, and the incorporated Standard
Contractual Clauses are deemed executed at the same moment. Requesting changes to the
DPA requires an Enterprise plan, so it is take-it-or-leave-it.

**Transfer mechanism:** EU and UK Standard Contractual Clauses with module-specific
variations for EEA, UK and Swiss transfers (§6), plus adherence to the
**EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework** (§11).

**Sub-processors:** <https://resend.com/legal/subprocessors>

### Data residency — the one real limitation

**Resend stores customer data in the United States. There is no EU region and no
data residency option.** The documentation path for data residency returns 404;
this is not a feature that exists on any plan.

This was assessed and accepted. Reasoning:

- GDPR does not require EU residency. It requires a lawful transfer mechanism, and
  SCCs plus EU-U.S. DPF adherence is exactly that.
- The data is minimal — an email address and a consent timestamp. No special
  category data, no financial data, no behavioural profile beyond open/click.
- The site already relies on US processors for comparable data: Formspree receives
  contact form submissions including message bodies, and Vercel handles hosting.
  Adding Resend does not change the site's transfer posture.

If EU residency later becomes a hard requirement, the migration path is
**Listmonk self-hosted on an EU VPS**, which was the only evaluated alternative
offering genuine EU-resident subscriber storage. The newsletter implementation
isolates all Resend calls in `api/_lib/resend.mjs` and `scripts/newsletter/broadcast.mjs`
specifically so that this remains a two-file change.

## Alternatives considered

Evaluated 2026-07-25, at which point the list was empty and cost was not yet a factor.

| Vendor | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| Kit (ConvertKit) | Free to 10,000 subscribers — far more generous — but it owns the signup form and subscriber database, and RSS campaigns require the Creator tier. Would replace the architecture rather than serve it. |
| beehiiv | Disqualified. Send API is Enterprise-only, making programmatic broadcast creation impossible at any reasonable price. |
| Buttondown | Has an API, but free tier stops at 100 subscribers and RSS-to-email is a paid add-on. |
| Loops | Free tier applies its own branding to emails. |
| Listmonk | Genuinely capable and EU-hostable, but requires running and patching Postgres plus a VPS. Disproportionate for a personal blog. |

**Deciding factor:** Resend is the only evaluated vendor serving both halves of the
design from one API. The double opt-in confirmation is a *transactional* send; the
newsletter is a *broadcast*. The marketing-first platforms handle transactional email
poorly or not at all.

**Known cost cliff:** broadcast pricing is per contact, not per send. Free to 1,000
contacts, then $40/month at 5,000, with no intermediate step. Revisit the comparison
above if the list approaches 1,000.
