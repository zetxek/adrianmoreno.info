Harari's book after Sapiens is a continuation and speculation about where the future of humanity might be headed towards. It delves into the potential trajectories of human evolution, technology, and society, exploring the ethical and philosophical implications of our choices today - and especially about how AI might shape society, and how its power can serve for global progress... or creating enormous inequality and concentration of power (economical and else).

A good reminder than today's decisions will shape the future, and that's not written yet.
