A guide for organizations on how to create a healthy and productive work culture. It rejects the "Silicon Valley" status quo and default ways of doing things, advocating for a more sane and people-centric approach.


Quite a germinal book for my way of thinking about work, and instilling the "things _can_ be done differently". 
It challenges the notion that long hours and constant busyness are necessary for success, promoting a more balanced and thoughtful approach to work. "Less is more", with a focus on quality over quantity, and defying the conventional wisdom that busyness equates to productivity.
