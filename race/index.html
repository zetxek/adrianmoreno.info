Direction should help teams make decisions without depending on one person.

Field notes / Principles

  1. Ship the org chart you need, not the one you inherited.

    Structure is a tool that follows strategy, not something to preserve for its own sake.

  2. Technical direction is a service to the team, not a trophy for the leader.

    I want the team to be able to explain an architecture decision without me in the room.

  3. Trust compounds faster than velocity.

    The trust that sustains delivery matters more than speed at the expense of the team.
